Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mindscape VR therapy

Do I need a VR headset to use Mindscape?

While a VR headset offers the most immersive experience, many therapies are available in cinematic mode on smartphones, tablets, or computers, delivering significant benefits.

How does VR therapy differ from traditional therapy?

VR therapy engages all senses in immersive environments, creating stronger neural pathways and enabling safe exposure therapy with real-time biofeedback.

Is Mindscape safe for children?

Programs for teens (13+) are available with parental guidance. For children under 13, consult a pediatric mental health professional before use.

What research supports Mindscape's VR therapy?

Over 300 studies show VR reduces anxiety by 40-70%, PTSD by 50-66%, and improves mindfulness by 30-50% compared to traditional methods.
